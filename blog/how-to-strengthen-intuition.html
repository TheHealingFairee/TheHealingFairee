How to Strengthen Your Intuition

February 10, 2025 • Written By Eliana Camargo

Everyone is intuitive. But Intuition is like a muscle, you can grow it by exercising it. With practice you’ll be able to easily discern what’s your intuitive voice, and have it speak to you more often. So how do we do this?

Lets first define exactly what I mean by intuition. Intuition is your gut instinct, like when you know that a situation or person is good or bad for you, without any external signs to give you a hint. Everyone knows that gut instinct is very real, but intuition goes even deeper than that! Intuition can also tie us to the knowledge of the universe. Whether that means communication with God or Angels, or if it means tapping into the oneness that is everything to gain insight about life, anyone can guidance from the universe. Intuition lets us gain glimpses of life from a birds eye view of the timeline of your past and future, like seeing your life from a higher perspective. It’s not just special fortune tellers who have this ability, everyone is a little bit psychic! Have you ever thought about someone, and 1 minute later they call you? How’d you know they were about to call? Or have a feeling you should check your email, and the message you’d been waiting for arrived exactly then? That’s your intuition, aka your 6th sense, or your 3rd eye.

First lets start with how to differentiate intuition from fear.

Our gut instinct will often tell us when a situation is bad for us, but sometimes what feels like intuition is just fear. If you’ve never learned how to distinguish between the two, it can make you lack trust in your gut. But just like you weren’t born knowing how to read, you can also be taught the difference between fear and intuition. It’s actually pretty simple, intuition will always feel peaceful, whereas fear comes up as an emotion. Even if the intuitive thought you get is about something bad, it’ll always come up peaceful, but then once your mind wraps around it you might start to feel panic afterwards. Whereas fear will immediately come up as panic.

Fear will make you feel agitated, anxious, or erratic. It brings uncertainty, makes you reminisce on past experiences, or catastrophize about the future. You’ll feel tense, your mind might race, and you could feel a heavy weight in your chest or stomach. Intuition, on the other hand, will feel like a sense of inner clarity, or a gentle pull in a particular direction. It’s a unique deep inner knowing. There's no urgency or anxiety attached to it. Instead, it’s steady, like a calm presence is whispering in your ear, guiding you from within. Not screaming in your ear like fear does.

For example, maybe you’re walking down the street and you feel a nudge that you should cross the street, for no particular reason. And then right as you cross, a tree falls where you would have been standing. That quiet knowing was your intuition.

So now that you know the difference between fear and intuition, how can you strengthen it so that you hear it more often and more clearly?

First: Meditate daily so you can tune into your inner voice. If you suck at meditating, I made a 5 minute beginner’s guided meditation that you can listen to right here! But the point of meditation is to become the observer of your thoughts, instead of getting carried down a rabbit hole of your thoughts. Meditation teaches you to come back to the present moment over and over. In order to hear your intuition you need a quiet mind, because intuition isn’t logic or thought based, it’s an inner knowing. It comes to you when you’ve cleared your mind and are in the present moment. The more you meditate, the better you’ll be at quieting your mind, so meditating daily is essential if you want to strengthen your intuition.

Second: When you get an intuitive hit, follow it! If you ignore your intuition it’ll learn that you don’t listen anyway, so why speak up? But the more you start following your intuition, the more it learns that it’s safe to be heard.

Third: Write down your intuitive thoughts and check to see if they come true. Then in the future when you feel something similar you’ll be able to tell if it’s your intuition or not! Over time, this strengthens your trust in your gut instincts and helps you recognize when your intuition really is speaking.

Fourth: If you’re not sure if something is your intuition, ask for guidance. Clear your mind and don’t search for the answer, just feel into it. If the answer is yes, typically you’ll feel light and expansive. And if it’s no you’ll feel compression or heavy.

Fifth: The more in tune you are with your emotions, the more intuitive you’ll be. That’s because the more aware you are of how your emotions feel, where they come from, and how they show up physically in your body, the easier it is to distinguish between the calm guidance of intuition from the nervous energy of fear.
